# A8 – Gears

## Objective

Design a gear box that can be assembled and disassembled. In the process analyze the size of input and output shafts as well as designing a journal bearing. Finally creating a CAD model of the gearbox.

Journal Bearing:


After I finished solving for the stress in my shafts. I started designing my journal bearing. I began by solving for P, V and PV for the input shaft. Before calculating i listed all my knowns and unknowns. 



<img width="388" height="274" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-27 210028" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/03b93cd3-abb6-4f04-baab-6201d84c9cb9" />


When I finished finding all my unknown values needed i followed the same process and calculations for the journal bearing used in my output shaft. 


<img width="377" height="317" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-27 210045" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c63717ce-b82f-4ad6-9ba3-ea61d36d522d" />



When my calculations where all done I  needed to choose a material for the bearings. I used the table in the handbook and decided to choose Phenolics as the material because the max for the load capacity, V max and PV are all bigger than what's needed and will give me a bigger safety factor.

Sketch:


Finally, before starting my Cad model i made a rough sketch of what my gear box will look like. I decided to make it a basic squared shape that will be held together by bold and nuts. I looked at many design but thought a basic squared box that's secured at each side would be good for this design.

Lessons learned:

I learned a lot during this assignment. I had never worked with anything i had import into solid works before so this taught me how to do it and get it into my model. I've also never assembled a multipart model before so this was my first time working with multiple pieces and having to put them all together to build the final piece. It gave me practice and taught me to be careful when assembling a model and be patience. Its better not to rush the process.
