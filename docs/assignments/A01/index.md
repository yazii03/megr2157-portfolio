## Analyze
Task A- Portfolio Analysis 

Portfolio #1
The engineering portfolio of the Engineer Javier Sanchez Ramirez hosted on Github is very easy to navigate, there are tabs in the portfolio that lead to different information about him. They allow his work and projects to be found quick and easy by anyone. However the work displayed in his portfolio does not include any documentation of his work process or any steps taken to achieve his final product. The only information about his work are pictures and one sentence descriptions of what he did. A colleague or anyone else would not be able to reproduce the work in his portfolio or know how he made decisions that led to his final product. The language used in his portfolio meets the standard language in a document that would be submitted to an employer there is just very limited information given about him and his projects. 

Portfolio #2
The engineering portfolio of the Engineer Nathaniel Karau hosted on Github has easy access to his project. A tab to his projects is on his portfolio twice making it easy to find for an employer or anyone interested in his work. His portfolio includes some his drawings, the tools and software he used to complete his project along with a 3d model and pictures of some of his project at different points of completion. However, there is no information about his decision making or written descriptions of his process while completing his projects which would make it difficult for a colleague to reproduce using solely what it is his portfolio and not asking any questions. The language used in his portfolio does meet the standard one would use in a document submitted to an employer, its also straight forward and organized for easy understanding.
