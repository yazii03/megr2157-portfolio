# A4 – Motor Mount

## Objective- Design a motor mount for a Brushed 24V DC Gear Motor 3.6 Kg.cm/46RPM. Create a 3D CAD model for the motor mount as well as a multi-view drawing. Document the process and show calculations. 


My first step was to choose a material to use for the deign of my motor mount. For this design I chose to use ABS as my material. I did some research and learned it is very strong and good with heat, it wont be as affected as my other options by heat.

I began by listing all of my unknowns and my known values. Values like the safety factor, force P as well as the max deflection were provided beforehand. I did some research on my material and found the Young Modulus as well as the yield strength that i will be using in my calculations. 


<img width="405" height="257" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-17 020334" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5bf585d8-a04c-41ea-b61e-6fca63ac5916" />


I first chose the length and base of my motor mount before doing any calculations since i would need it to solve anything any further. I chose to make the base and the length of the first feature 33mm. I chose 33 mm because the diameter of the gear motor is 28 mm, I didn't want the motor mount to have the same measurements as the motor so I added some extra space.

I created an FBD to show my dimensions better. I used my length and my given force of 300N to solve for the moment in feature 1. 


<img width="329" height="299" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-17 020727" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/41dc0699-7215-4ddf-aa54-5ba03eca70af" />


With my moment calculated I followed by solving for the height of my first part of the motor mount. I used the beam bending equations showed in lecture to find "h" both with max stress and max deflection. I picked the largest found value to continue to use in my future calculations for this assignment.
